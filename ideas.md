**Idea 1: Industrial Legal Modernism**

- **Design Movement**: Brutalist-Modernist hybrid — legal gravitas meets crypto-native rawness
- **Core Principles**:
  1. Dark, near-black canvas with brass/gold as the sole accent — authority without decoration
  2. Monospace + serif type pairing — machine precision meets legal tradition
  3. Grid overlay as structural metaphor — the blockchain as infrastructure
  4. Every element earns its place — no decorative filler, only functional beauty
- **Color Philosophy**: Near-black (#080808) as the void, brass (#c9a84c) as value and trust, cream (#f2efe8) as legibility. The palette evokes aged legal documents, gold seals, and the cold precision of code.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Left-anchored, asymmetric flow. Hero text bleeds to the left edge. Sections use full-bleed dividers with numbered headers. No centered hero — content anchors bottom-left like a legal brief.
- **Signature Elements**:
  1. Rotating circular stamp (like a notary seal) in hero
  2. Brass left-border accent bars on cards
  3. Scrolling ticker bar showing chain data
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Minimal, purposeful. Hover reveals brass accent bars. Buttons translate on hover. No gratuitous animation — only motion that communicates state.
- **Animation**: Fade-up entrance animations, rotating stamp, scrolling ticker. Subtle and deliberate.
- **Typography System**: Syne 800 for headings (geometric authority), DM Mono for body/UI (machine precision), Instrument Serif italic for emphasis (legal tradition)
</text>

- **Signature Elements**:
  1. Rotated red circle stamp in hero

  3. Code blocks styled as legal exhibits
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Scroll-driven, document-like. Sections reveal like turning pages. Minimal interactivity — the document IS the experience.
- **Animation**: Subtle page-turn effects, stamp rotation on load, smooth scroll between sections.
- **Typography System**: EB Garamond for body (legal document tradition), Bebas Neue for section numbers (bold contrast), Courier Prime for code/metadata (typewriter authenticity)

## Selected Design: **Industrial Legal Modernism** (Idea 1)

This approach best captures AgentCorp's dual identity: the legal gravitas of Delaware entity formation and the crypto-native precision of Base blockchain infrastructure. The dark canvas with brass accents creates immediate authority, while the Syne/DM Mono/Instrument Serif type system communicates both machine precision and legal tradition.
